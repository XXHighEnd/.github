## First Look at XXHighEnd

XXHighEnd is for people who keep a DAC they own and want one named xx desk, not mixed untitled engines. It gives you own tracks, a dac you own, and a short path back to a bit path you already use. It does not replace the rest of your PC.

Work stays on tracks you own. Extra engines wait for their own title.
